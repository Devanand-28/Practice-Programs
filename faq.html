Frequently Asked Questions

The things customers ask us most — tap a question to see the answer.

Do you offer home delivery?
Yes, for orders in and around our KRR Nagar / Vadaveeranaickenpatty neighbourhood in Theni. Call or WhatsApp us your list on the number on our Contact page and we'll let you know the delivery time and any minimum order amount.
What are your store timings?
We're open every day of the week in two sessions: 6:30 AM – 2:30 PM in the morning, and 4:30 PM – 10:30 PM in the evening. We're closed for a short break between 2:30 PM and 4:30 PM.
What payment methods do you accept?
Cash and all major UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) are accepted at the counter. Just ask the person billing you for our UPI QR code if you don't see it displayed.
Do you take bulk or wholesale orders?
Yes — for functions, events, or larger household stock-ups, contact us ahead of time through the Contact page or by phone so we can set aside quantities and confirm pricing for bulk purchases.
Can I run a monthly account (khata) with the store?
Regular customers can speak with the shop owner directly about setting up a monthly account. This is arranged in person and isn't something we can confirm over the website.
How often do your prices change?
We update the Products page whenever rates change, so what you see there reflects our current prices. Vegetable and fruit prices can shift with the market more often than packaged goods.
An item I need isn't listed on the Products page — do you still stock it?
Possibly — the Products page lists our common items, but our shelves carry more than what's listed online. Call ahead or drop by and ask; we're happy to check.
Where exactly are you located?
We're in KRR Nagar, NRT Nagar, Vadaveeranaickenpatty, Theni, Tamil Nadu 625531. See the Location page for the map and directions.

Reach out directly and we'll get back to you.
