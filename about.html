Our Story

About Sivamurugan Stores

A local shop built on fresh stock, fair prices, and knowing our customers by name.

Sivamurugan Stores sits in the KRR Nagar area of Theni, where it has grown into a dependable stop for households nearby. What started as a small counter of everyday essentials has become a full neighbourhood store — vegetables, groceries, snacks, stationery and household goods, all within easy reach.

The shop is run day to day by the family that owns it, which means the person weighing your vegetables or ringing up your bill is usually the same person who restocked the shelves that morning. That closeness is what shapes how the store is run: honest weighing, fair pricing, and stock that's replaced often rather than left to sit.

What we care about

  • 01

  • 02
